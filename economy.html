OCTOBER 1 · NEW REWARD SOURCES

Get paid as you progress.

October 1 release notes: These describe the new server update. Live installation and the Assistant command fix are awaiting confirmation; use the in-game menus and Discord for rollout status.

Advancement money

Visible Tasks pay $75, Goals $150, and Challenges $300. Previously completed eligible advancements are scanned once. Internal technical advancements are excluded.

Campaign mission cash

151 one-time requirement rewards total $76,150 across 31 stages, in addition to the existing milestone payouts. Completed stages are included in the mission-cash backtrack.

These are Dollar rewards, not Tokens or Jobs+ Job Coins. Read all release notes.
