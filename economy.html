What each reward actually pays

Campaign mission cash and stage rewards are separate payments. Mission cash pays once per requirement when checked. Stages 1–30 pay once; Stage 31 pays cash, Tokens and its item bundle on every successful Ascension. Stage 31 mission cash still pays once. Dollars, Tokens, Jobs+ Job Coins and seasonal Spirit are separate balances.

151 mission rewards total $76,150 once. Stages 1–30 add $3,223,000 + 1,317 Tokens. Stage 31 adds $250,000 + 125 Tokens per successful Ascension. Mission cash can arrive before the stage claim; queued cash may appear as one combined deposit.
