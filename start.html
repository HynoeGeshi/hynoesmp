START HERE

Your first day should create momentum.

This is the recommended route from fresh spawn to a stable Hynoe SMP character. Hynoe does not use vanilla unlock pacing: Genesis Ages open parts of the game over time and through milestones. Check things off as you understand them — the list saves in your browser.

NEW WORLD // FIRST SUNSET
YOUR IN-GAME GUIDE

New here? Type /help first.

You do not need to memorize the whole server. Open Minecraft chat and type /help to open the simple guide. When you need an explanation, ask a question. When you need a goal, open your next campaign stage.

/help
Learn where to start. Open the simple server guide when you first join or need a refresher.
/ask <question>
Get server guidance. Replace <question> with your own words—do not type the brackets. For example: /ask where do I find a Farseer. Use it for questions about server systems or finding a mob.
/next
Find your next goal. Open your current campaign stage to see what to work on.
/quests
Check progress and claim rewards. Review the exact stage requirements, see what is left, and use the campaign menu to check and claim.
/season
Explore the current event. Open the seasonal hub for the active phase and its objectives.
/tips off
/tips on
Choose how much guidance you see. Turn optional tips off, or turn them back on when you want reminders.

A simple first-session routine: /help to get oriented → /next to pick a goal → /ask followed by your question if you get stuck → /quests to check and claim your progress.

Command not found? The server answered /ask and invoked the current campaign stage through /next during the October 7 console check. Use /quests for the campaign and ask in Discord if a command is unavailable.

FIRST 20 MINUTES

Secure the basics.

Get wood, food, torches, a bed if possible, and a safe place for storage. Do not panic when familiar recipes or tiers are locked — Genesis is intentionally controlling the early-game pace.

LEARN THE LOCKS

Genesis Ages decide what your character is ready for.

Tool tiers unlock by actually wearing out the previous tier. Smelting waits for 5 in-game days of playtime. Copper armor waits for 10. Story milestones control major dimension access, including The End.

WOOD → STONE → COPPER → IRON → DIAMOND → NETHERITESee every Genesis Age →
PROGRESS WITHOUT SKIPPING

Build a recovery plan as each Age opens.

Keep spare food and unlocked tools at base so one death never erases the whole session. Follow the Genesis tool chain instead of trying to jump straight to iron or diamond, and build armor around the Age your character has actually reached.

TRAVEL FROM DAY ONE

Use RTP, TPA, and Waystones.

/rtp finds new territory. /tpa <player> requests a visit; the other player uses /tpaccept or /tpdeny. Waystones are always unlocked. Save your first base with /sethome base. Open the Homes menu with /home or /homes; /home base teleports directly. You start with one home, then unlock up to four additional campaign homes.

PROTECT WHAT MATTERS

Plan safe storage and shared builds.

Keep spare gear and valuable items organized. Agree on boundaries and access with neighbors and teammates. The old claims system was removed; report griefing or theft to staff.

LEARN THE NETWORK

Dollars. Tokens. Jobs+.

Dollars handle normal trade. Tokens reward progression and buy curated Vault rewards. Jobs+ handles profession specialization.
