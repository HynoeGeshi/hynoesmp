START HERE

Your first day should create momentum.

This is the recommended route from fresh spawn to a stable Hynoe SMP character. Hynoe does not use vanilla unlock pacing: Genesis Ages open parts of the game over time and through milestones. Check things off as you understand them — the list saves in your browser.

NEW WORLD // FIRST SUNSET
YOUR IN-GAME GUIDE

New here? Type /help first.

You do not need to memorize the whole server. Open Minecraft chat and type /help to open the simple guide. When you need an explanation, ask a question. When you need a goal, open your next campaign stage.

/help
Learn where to start. Open the simple server guide when you first join or need a refresher.
/ask <question>
Get server guidance. Replace <question> with your own words—do not type the brackets. For example: /ask where do I find a Farseer. Answers come from a curated guide to server systems and mob locations. Use /quests to check your personal progress.
/next
Find your next goal. Open your current campaign stage to see what to work on.
/quests
Check progress and claim rewards. Review the exact stage requirements, see what is left, and use the campaign menu to check and claim.
/season
Explore the current event. Open the seasonal hub for the active phase and its objectives.
/claims
Protect your build. Open the native Claims menu for claiming, unclaiming, personal totals and settings. Read the Claims guide.
/tips off
/tips on
Choose how much guidance you see. Turn optional tips off, or turn them back on when you want reminders.

A simple first-session routine: /help to get oriented → /next to pick a goal → /ask followed by your question if you get stuck → /quests to check and claim your progress.

Need more help? Assistant v1.2.3 improves question matching. Use /quests for the campaign and ask in Discord if a command or guide answer does not help.

FIRST 20 MINUTES

Secure the basics.

Get wood, food, torches, a bed if possible, and a safe place for storage. Do not panic when familiar recipes or tiers are locked — Genesis is intentionally controlling the early-game pace.

LEARN THE LOCKS

Genesis Ages decide what your character is ready for.

Tool tiers unlock by actually wearing out the previous tier. Smelting waits for 5 in-game days of playtime. Copper armor waits for 10. Story milestones control major dimension access, including The End.

WOOD → STONE → COPPER → IRON → DIAMOND → NETHERITESee every Genesis Age →
PROGRESS WITHOUT SKIPPING

Build a recovery plan as each Age opens.

Keep spare food and unlocked tools at base so one death never erases the whole session. Follow the Genesis tool chain instead of trying to jump straight to iron or diamond, and build armor around the Age your character has actually reached.

TRAVEL FROM DAY ONE

Use RTP, TPA, and Waystones.

/rtp finds new territory. /tpa <player> requests a visit; the other player uses /tpaccept or /tpdeny. Waystones are always unlocked. Save your first base with /sethome base. Open the Homes menu with /home or /homes; /home base teleports directly. You start with one home, then unlock up to four additional campaign homes.

PROTECT WHAT MATTERS

Claim your base and check access.

Stand in the chunk you want to protect and open /claims. Check your claim limits and personal or party settings before sharing access. Agree on boundaries with neighbors and report griefing or theft to staff.

CLAIMS · OCTOBER 14, 2026

Protect your build from the Claims menu.

Type /claims to open the native Claims dialog. You can also use the clickable Claims button in the welcome chat. The sidebar shows the command as a reminder; type it in chat to open the menu. Keep using your current client pack—no new download is needed.

/claims
Open the Claims menu.
/claim
Claim the chunk you are standing in using the existing /oclaims claim action.
/unclaim
Open a confirmation for the chunk where you started the action. Review it before confirming removal.
/claiminfo
See your personal claim count and limits.
/claimsettings
Open personal or party main-claim settings. Party changes require the appropriate existing role.

Choose access in settings.

Doors, Chests and Barrels offer Normal rules or Also allies. Normal rules removes the extra access for that category. Existing Full Access permissions, including party access, still apply. Raid settings offer Allow, Block or Read.

Existing claim modes, limits, required distance, dimension restrictions and role protections still apply. Check the menu's response if an action is refused.

FALL 2026 · COMPLETION GUIDE

Complete all eight Fall tasks.

Open /season first to join, then use its objectives and Claim buttons. Breeding, biome visits and hunt credit must happen after joining. Existing progress stays saved as the phase changes. Each core reward is once per player.

After Dark opened on October 31, 2026 at 12:00 a.m. EDT and is active. Homecoming opens automatically on November 7, 2026 at 12:00 a.m. EST. Both times use America/New_York. If the server is offline at midnight, activation retries during that date; check /season for the active phase.

Finish and claim all four After Dark tasks before November 7 at 12:00 a.m. EST. Homecoming closes dark hunts and dark claims, even if you already earned their requirements. The three Harvest tasks remain available through Homecoming.

Spirit milestones give collectibles, with no extra Tokens or Dollars. Core claims, four relics and three biome visits already total 106 Spirit, enough for the 100-Spirit milestone.

Use and display your seasonal rewards.

Lanterns, trophies, relics, charms, the compass and the crown are decorative collectibles. Place an Item Frame or Glow Item Frame on a floor, wall or ceiling, then right-click it with the reward. They display in the frame and return as items when removed. Use a spot where your claim permissions allow building. Lanterns do not emit light and bells do not ring; the compass and crown are display collectibles.

  • Harvestkeeper’s Scythe: use it as a netherite hoe to till soil and harvest hoe-mineable blocks.
  • Duskblade: a diamond sword with Smite V for fighting undead mobs.
  • Nightfall Totem: hold it in either hand for normal fatal-damage protection; it is consumed when it saves you.
  • Homecoming Satchel: use it as a normal bundle. Hold it on your cursor: left-click an inventory stack to store items, then right-click an empty slot to remove them. Its capacity is the normal bundle limit, not 64 separate tools.

All 19 Fall reward icons use built-in Minecraft artwork, with no extra resource-pack download. To repair an old missing icon, carry the reward in your main inventory or offhand and reopen /season. Take items out of chests, backpacks or bundles first. Names, enchantments, item identity and claim history are preserved. Use /ask season rewards in game for these directions.

Find the four relics.

Get player kill credit while enrolled in the right phase. Frostglass and Murmur’s Bell can start in Longer Nights, if that phase is active, and continue in After Dark; Tusklin Moonfang and Skreecher Echo require After Dark. Each has an early-drop chance and a guaranteed award by the listed kill count.

  • Frostglass: Froststalkers in Ice Spikes or Frozen Peaks, on suitable ground in daylight. Guaranteed by 20 credited kills.
  • Murmur’s Bell: Murmurs in dark Overworld caves at Y −30 or lower, away from the Deep Dark. Guaranteed by 20 credited kills.
  • Tusklin Moonfang: Tusklins in Ice Spikes or Biomes O’ Plenty Snowblossom Grove, on suitable ground in daylight. Guaranteed by 25 credited kills.
  • Skreecher Echo: Skreechers in dark Deep Dark caverns with sculk beneath their spawn area. Guaranteed by 25 credited kills. Their Warden interaction remains active.

A Nature’s Compass helps find biomes; an Explorer’s Compass helps find structures. Wither Skeletons spawn in Nether Fortresses; installed Dark Temple, Nether Monsterroom and Nether Ship templates also contain their spawners. Hunt where claim settings permit it. For the Void Worm, process Crimson Mosquito Larva in a Capsid, then throw the resulting Mysterious Worm into the End void below Y −60. Summoning alone does not count as a kill. Use /ask void worm or /ask skreecher for more directions.

Prepare the Homecoming food.

The installed Croptopia recipes use: Candy Corn = 2 Sugar + Corn; Pumpkin Spice Latte = Sugar + Paprika + Milk + Pumpkin + Coffee; Pecan Pie = Pecans + Sugar + Egg + Flour + Dough + Frying Pan. Use the client recipe viewer for ingredient alternatives and supporting recipes.

Overworld starting points for biomes and hunts.

Use these server-located starting points, or search with Nature’s Compass:

  • Maple Woods: X -32, Z -288.
  • Orchard: X 1280, Z 1120.
  • Seasonal Forest: X -96, Z -288.
  • Ice Spikes: X -352, Z -4896, for daylight Froststalker and Tusklin hunts.
  • Deep Dark: near X -320, Y -63, Z -672, for Skreecher exploration.

Ground height and cave terrain vary. Travel with suitable gear, explore the surrounding biome, and stay briefly for the visit check. Hostile mobs still need their normal spawning conditions; reaching a biome alone does not complete a hunt.

Bring the items. Refresh the count.

Harvest and Homecoming item counts show what is in your carried main inventory, not lifetime collection totals. Chests and backpacks do not count. Keep at least seven main-inventory slots empty before claiming, so the inventory guard and rewards have room.

For enrolled players online, counts and biome discoveries refresh about every five seconds at normal server speed. Stay briefly in each target biome. An open menu is a snapshot: use Refresh Counts or reopen it after your inventory changes. Keep using your current client pack.

SILK TOUCH

Moving a normal spawner.

Normal spawner pickup is enabled and requires Silk Touch. Trial Spawners are excluded. Pickup and placement with the original mob type still await an in-game player check; report unexpected results before moving more spawners.
