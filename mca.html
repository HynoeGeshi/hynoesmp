VILLAGE GUIDEMIRA

“Start with the person, not the menu. Every villager reacts differently.”

▼
01
RELATIONSHIPS

Relationships are individual.

Personality and current mood can change how conversations, jokes, stories, hugs, flirting, and gifts are received. Repeating the same interaction can become less effective, so read the response instead of spamming the menu.

Every relationship has its own story.
02
LIFE MAP

Give every resident a place in town.

⌂HOME

Where they return and sleep.

⚒WORKPLACE

Where daily work is centered.

✦HANGOUT

Where free time happens.

Follow Me, Stay Here, Move Freely, and Go Home help turn random villagers into a controlled settlement.

03
FAMILY

Marriage changes the settlement.

Relationships can progress into engagement, marriage, children, and eventually multiple generations. Children grow through life stages and can become adults with lives of their own.

04
PROFESSIONS + DEFENSE

Build districts, not workstation clutter.

Librarians, Farmers, Smiths, Clerics, and other professions still depend on workstations and safe access. As a village matures, advanced progression can support guards, archers, village rules, and stronger settlement management.

05
LEGACY

Loss has history.

MCA includes systems around villager death, graves, and revival-related progression. Build walls, lighting, safe paths, protected sleeping zones, and recovery plans before your population becomes large.
