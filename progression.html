PROGRESSION ROADMAP

Power should feel earned.

Hynoe SMP is balanced around having meaningful goals left weeks and months later. Genesis controls the early unlocks, then the server-specific gear ladder takes over. Power is one road through the world — not a skip button for the rest of it.

DEEP PROGRESSION // RARE ORES AHEAD
THE HYNOE LOOP

Everything feeds the next adventure.

Survive→Explore→Earn→Upgrade→Fight→Build
GENESIS // AGE SYSTEM

Your character has to earn access to the world.

Genesis sits underneath Hynoe SMP's gear ladder. Some unlocks are based on time played; others require you to use a previous tier, clear a challenge, or complete a Story Age. That is why a recipe, item, furnace, armor set, or dimension can still be unavailable even when you know it exists.

CORE RULE OWNING IT ≠ UNLOCKING IT

Progress the Age itself. Do not assume another player handing you an item will bypass the gate.

WHY THIS EXISTS

Hynoe SMP is supposed to have a beginning, middle, and endgame. Genesis prevents a lucky chest, rich friend, or fast mining route from deleting the early game before you have actually played it.
