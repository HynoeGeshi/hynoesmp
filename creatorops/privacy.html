Hynoe CreatorOps

Privacy notice

Effective October 6, 2026 · Prototype version

What we collect

When you submit a Creator Audit, we collect the name you provide, email address, primary platform, channel/profile URL, goals, bottlenecks, selected revenue range, and the service you are interested in. For abuse prevention, the server also stores a one-way hash of the browser user-agent string rather than the raw value.

What we do not ask for

Do not send passwords, recovery codes, API keys, access tokens, payment card numbers, or other login secrets. Future account connections should use the provider's official authorization flow whenever supported.

How the data is used

Application data is used to review your creator operation, prepare an audit, qualify service fit, manage follow-up, and operate the CreatorOps prototype. Normalized Stripe sandbox event data may be stored to verify that the payment workflow works; raw card data is handled by Stripe and is not stored by CreatorOps.

Storage and access

CreatorOps is hosted on Render and stores application/payment records in Supabase. Browser roles do not have direct database access. The public Render service validates requests and uses signed server-to-server requests to reach the database gateway. The operator console is separately authenticated.

Retention

Prototype records are kept only as long as reasonably needed for testing, follow-up, security, or an active client relationship and may be removed manually. A production retention schedule will be finalized before live billing is enabled.

Sharing

We do not sell Creator Audit application data. Infrastructure providers may process data only as necessary to host, secure, or operate the service.

Your choices

You may ask Hynoe to correct or delete an application through the CreatorOps contact/application channel. Do not include sensitive credentials in that request.
