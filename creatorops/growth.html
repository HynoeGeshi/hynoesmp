The Hynoe method

One offer. One clear test.
No guessing disguised as analytics.

01 / FIND THE EVIDENCE

Research before recommendations

Review public profiles, link paths, positioning and relevant official sources. Add owner-authorized analytics where available. Label every number as verified, reported, assumed or unavailable.

02 / PACKAGE THE VALUE

Sell something deliverable

Define one audience-fit offer, its contents, price, dates, capacity, access and refund terms. Test demand with paid orders—not comments or income promises.

03 / PREPARE THE CAMPAIGN

Give each post a job

Use rights-cleared recordings for hooks, useful moments and offer previews. Draft captions, livestream segments and a clear call to action. The creator approves final posts and scheduling.

04 / REMOVE THE FRICTION

Make the next step obvious

Give the approved offer a prominent link. Check the mobile path and checkout. Use source tags only where the destination records them; do not claim attribution that was not measured.

05 / TEST, THEN SCALE

Use the first orders as evidence

Choose a small validation target before launch. Inspect distribution when visits are low; inspect the offer and checkout when visits do not become purchases. Do not buy ads blindly.

06 / RECONCILE THE MONEY

Sales are not bank cash

Record product sales, platform earnings, fees, refunds, fulfillment costs, pending funds and settled deposits separately. Close only genuine deadlines. Deliver the promised value.
