Privacy at Hynoe

Hynoe is building a worldwide Minecraft community and an original browser game, Hynoe Outpost. This notice explains the information the current website uses and the privacy limits we are intentionally keeping in place while account features are still being built.

Current account status: Cloud accounts and cloud saves are not live. Hynoe Outpost can be played locally without giving Hynoe an email address. The site-wide Hall of Legends can be viewed by everyone; publishing your own score is optional and off by default.

1. What the current site uses

Browser-local game and guide data

Hynoe Outpost and parts of the site guide use browser storage such as localStorage to remember game progress, preferences, guide progress, a local callsign/player identifier, and similar state on your device. This information stays in your browser unless a feature clearly says it will send information elsewhere.

Deleting your browser/site data can remove this local progress. See Data Deletion before clearing it if you want to understand the effect.

Technical request information

Like most websites, hosting, security, DNS, content-delivery, and service providers may process ordinary technical request information such as IP address, request time, requested URL, browser or device information, and security logs as needed to deliver and protect the site. Hynoe does not ask you to submit precise location, government ID, biometrics, or contact lists to play Hynoe Outpost locally.

Global leaderboard

The site-wide Hall of Legends can load public ranking data from Hynoe's leaderboard service. Merely viewing the board does not upload your Hynoe Outpost save or gameplay metrics. Publishing your own ranking is optional and off by default.

If you turn on Publish my score, the site may send a random local player ID, your chosen callsign, and bounded gameplay metrics used to calculate/rank your Legend score. You can turn publication off to stop future score uploads. To request review or deletion of an existing leaderboard record, use Data Deletion. Standard technical request information may still be processed by the service provider when the public board is loaded.

Website chat

The website chat relay is not connected at this time. The page may still show chat UI, but the current configuration does not provide an active relay URL. If website chat is enabled later, this notice will be updated before Hynoe relies on that feature for personal-data processing.

Video and outside links

The Watch & Play page can load a YouTube privacy-enhanced video player after you choose to play a stream. Hynoe also links to services such as Discord, YouTube, Streamlabs, Modrinth, and other third-party sites. Those services have their own terms and privacy practices once you visit or use them.

2. Emails and cloud saves

Hynoe does not currently operate the planned email cloud-save system. Before any cloud-save email field is enabled, Hynoe plans to publish the actual provider/data flow, age controls, retention approach, access/export/deletion process, and security controls that apply to that system.

The initial cloud-account plan is 18+ only. Users under 18 can continue using local-only Hynoe Outpost without providing an email address. Hynoe does not need your full date of birth for the current local game.

3. Children and younger users

Hynoe is aware that Minecraft and online games can be accessed by younger players. The local-only game is therefore designed to work without an account. Hynoe will not intentionally use an under-18 cloud-account path at initial launch. If Hynoe learns that personal information has been collected in a way that requires a child-privacy response, Hynoe will evaluate the record and take the deletion, consent, restriction, or other action required by applicable law.

4. Why information is used

  • To deliver, operate, secure, debug, and improve the website and game.
  • To keep local game/guide state on your device when you choose to use those features.
  • To prevent abuse and protect Hynoe systems when security logs are necessary.
  • For a feature you deliberately activate, only as described when that feature is enabled.

Hynoe does not operate behavioral advertising on this site at this time and does not intentionally sell personal data through the Hynoe site.

5. Retention

Browser-local Hynoe data generally remains on your device until it is cleared by you, the browser, or the site. Third-party infrastructure providers may retain technical logs according to their operational/security policies and Hynoe's service configuration. Hynoe's goal for future account systems is to keep identifiable data only as long as needed for the disclosed purpose, legal/security obligations, or a valid dispute.

6. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to information, access, correction, deletion, objection/restriction, or portability. Hynoe intends to honor applicable rights and will build electronic rights workflows before worldwide cloud accounts are enabled.

For current local data, see Data Deletion. For a private question about legacy server-side data, use the official Hynoe Discord linked from this site and ask to contact the Hynoe owner/moderation team privately. Do not post a player identifier, email address, legal name, or other private information in a public Discord channel.

7. International users

Hynoe is based in the United States but is intended to be used worldwide. Privacy laws can depend on where the user lives and what a feature does. Hynoe therefore uses a conservative worldwide product baseline and will reassess legal requirements before launching cloud accounts, under-18 accounts, targeted advertising, new analytics, or additional social-data features.

8. Security

Hynoe uses security controls intended to reduce unauthorized access or disclosure, but no online service can promise perfect security. Private/server credentials should never be present in public browser code. Suspected security problems should be reported privately as described in the repository security policy or through the official Hynoe Discord.

9. Changes to this notice

This notice will be updated when Hynoe materially changes its data practices. The date at the top shows the current version. Features that materially increase personal-data collection should not be enabled before the notice describes them.
