BEFORE EVERY RITUAL

Boss protocol

  1. Repair gear and unload unrelated valuables.
  2. Stage food, healing, spare equipment, and a safe return route.
  3. Know exactly how the encounter starts before touching the ritual.
  4. Never summon destructive bosses beside homes, villages, farms, or public infrastructure.
  5. For teams, agree on loot ownership and retreat conditions before the fight.
