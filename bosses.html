SHRINE TROUBLESHOOTING

Found a shrine that will not start?

Check that the Totem matches the shrine and use the intended ritual point. If nothing happens, send staff the dimension, shrine type, and coordinates. Shrine spawning and activation have caused issues on the server, so do not treat a locator result as proof that an encounter is working.

BEFORE EVERY RITUAL

Boss protocol

  1. Repair gear and unload unrelated valuables.
  2. Stage food, healing, spare equipment, and a safe return route.
  3. Know exactly how the encounter starts before touching the ritual.
  4. Never summon destructive bosses beside homes, villages, farms, or public infrastructure.
  5. For teams, agree on loot ownership and retreat conditions before the fight.
