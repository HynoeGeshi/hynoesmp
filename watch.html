    START HERE / 60-SECOND FIELD GUIDE

    Why am I clicking?

    Every click grows the same outpost. Tap for spendable ore, open veins for crafting materials, and use both to reach collectible crew, exploration, and permanent power.

    1. ⛏
      1. Tap one vein until it opensEach tap gives ore. The final tap also gives bonus ore, one matching material, and progress toward caches and bosses.
    2. ⚙
      2. Spend ore in WorkshopPickaxes strengthen every tap. Miners and machines create ore automatically, including while you are away.
    3. ♛
      3. Earn and equip crew cardsNormal play fills crew caches. Equipped cards boost strikes, idle income, and cave supplies across the whole game.
    4. ⌖
      4. Explore for permanent rewardsCampaign goals, cave choices, Guardians, research, gear, and relics all make future runs stronger.
    5. ✦
      5. Legacy when the meter fillsReset basic ore and machines for +25% power. Crew, research, gear, relics, discoveries, and achievements stay.
