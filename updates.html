OCTOBER 14 · CLAIMS

Your claims, in one native menu.

Type /claims or use the clickable Claims button in the welcome chat to open the native Claims dialog. Claim your current chunk, review your personal count and limits, or open personal and party main-claim settings. Unclaiming opens a confirmation for the original chunk.

Doors, Chests and Barrels offer Normal rules or Also allies. Normal rules removes the extra access for that category. Existing Full Access permissions, including party access, still apply. Raid settings offer Allow, Block or Read. Existing claim modes, limits, required distance, dimension restrictions and role protections still apply.

No new client download is needed. Claims commands and settings →

OCTOBER 8 · SERVER UPDATE

Fall progress that matches what you carry.

Seasonal item counts now track your carried main inventory correctly. Counts refresh every five seconds for enrolled players who are online and when the journal opens. An open menu is a snapshot: use Refresh Counts or reopen it after collecting items. Biome discoveries are checked every five seconds.

  • Fall rewards: Newly awarded items affected by missing models now use built-in fallback visuals. Items already stored are not automatically converted. The eight core claims and completion reward still total 30 Tokens.
  • Autumn leaves: particles appear randomly five to eight blocks overhead where there is open air and sky above an active player.
  • Better /ask answers: clearer question matching avoids unrelated Homes and generic item replies. Answers come from a curated server guide; use /quests for your personal progress.
  • Normal spawners: Silk Touch pickup is enabled. Read the pickup limits and current status.

These server updates need no new client download. How seasonal counts work

October 1: rewards along the way

151 mission rewards

Each tracked campaign requirement gains one-time cash. Together they total $76,150 across all 31 stages. Existing stage payouts remain the larger milestones.

31 stage bundles

Stages 1–30 award their themed or utility bundle once. Stage 31 awards its bundle on every successful Ascension; its mission cash remains one-time.

$75 / $150 / $300

Visible normal and modded advancements pay by category: Task, Goal, and Challenge. Internal Hynoe technical advancements do not pay.

Earlier progress counts

The update backtracks completed stages for new mission cash and scans eligible completed advancements once. Reconnecting or rechecking is not intended to pay the same reward again.

Campaign screens that explain themselves

  • Clearer current / required progress across all 31 stages.
  • TURN IN (CONSUMED) means the items are handed over when the complete claim succeeds. HAVE, KEEP, and DO requirements are not consumed.
  • Use the claim buttons in the live quest menus, including Stage 31.
  • An Assistant button on every stage and location guidance for rare-mob objectives.

Use /quests for actual progress, exact requirements, and available rewards.
