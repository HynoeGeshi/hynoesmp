Campaign v15 · Hynoe Assistant v1.0.1 · Handbook v9 · Fall v2.1

October 1 release notes: These describe the new server update. Live installation and the Assistant command fix are awaiting confirmation; use the in-game menus and Discord for rollout status.

JOIN HYNOE SMPPLAYER COMMANDS

Rewards along the way

151 mission rewards

Each tracked campaign requirement gains one-time cash. Together they total $76,150 across all 31 stages. Existing stage payouts remain the larger milestones.

31 stage bundles

Every stage gains a one-time themed or utility item bundle. The new bonus is not repeatedly claimable, including on the repeatable endgame stage.

$75 / $150 / $300

Visible normal and modded advancements pay by category: Task, Goal, and Challenge. Internal Hynoe technical advancements do not pay.

Earlier progress counts

The update backtracks completed stages for new mission cash and scans eligible completed advancements once. Reconnecting or rechecking is not intended to pay the same reward again.

Campaign screens that explain themselves

  • Clearer current / required progress across all 31 stages.
  • TURN IN (CONSUMED) means the items are handed over when the complete claim succeeds. HAVE, KEEP, and DO requirements are not consumed.
  • CHECK + CLAIM STAGE wording is consistent, including Stage 31.
  • An Assistant button on every stage and location guidance for rare-mob objectives.

Use /quests for actual progress, exact requirements, and available rewards.

Less guessing. More playing.

The handbook is a reference when you need it. Start with help or ask a plain-language question. The v1.0.1 follow-up addresses the reported /ask and /next registration problem; the server must restart with the fixed Assistant before those commands can be checked.

/help
Open the simple guide.
/ask <question>
Ask about server systems or finding a mob. Try “/ask where do I find a Farseer”.
/next
Open your current campaign stage.
/quests
Check and claim campaign progress.
/season
Open the seasonal hub.
/tips off / /tips on
Control optional tips that rotate about every five minutes.
/token / /tbal
Open the Token Vault or check your Token balance.
/auction
Open the Auction House.

If /ask or /next is unavailable, use /quests and ask for help in Discord while the fixed Assistant rollout is confirmed.

FALL v2.1

Your season, with clearer progress.

Harvest and Homecoming turn-ins display live inventory counts. Breeding, kills, biomes, and relic objectives show their live counts too. Hidden hunts gain clearer mob-finding guidance.

  1. Harvest
  2. Longer Nights
  3. After Dark
  4. Homecoming

This is the phase-number key, not a calendar or a claim that all four phases are open. Check /season for the active phase. All eight existing v2.0 claim payouts are unchanged, and the v2.0 Fall resource pack stays the same.

Returning player?

No additional client mod is required for this server-side overhaul. Keep using the Hynoe SMP client pack. Open /quests for your campaign and /season for the current seasonal objectives.

The website’s mining game has separate browser progress; its rewards do not become server money or Tokens. Minecraft-to-website chat is still awaiting connection.
